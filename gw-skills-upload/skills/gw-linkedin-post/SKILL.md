# GW LinkedIn post (framework)
Platform rules: blueprints/linkedin.md; patterns: blueprints/swipe/linkedin.md. The copywriter delivers the framework; `gw-linkedin-editor` writes the final version.

## Steps
1. **Slot:** personal (2 a day) or company page (at least 1 a day, in Brennan's voice, a different angle from the same day's personal posts); Pulse is 1 a week on a BOFU keyword.
2. **Idea:** industry mix per team/industry-mix.md (Oct 16 rotation: fencing, HVAC, roofing, siding, electrical, plumbing, other home services; at most 1-2 of every 5 on fencing), the 75% value / 25% lead-gen mix, and blog cut-downs via `gw-blog-to-social`.
3. **Keyword-led opening** (after the @Gorilla Works Marketing mention): what the post is about, in plain words.
4. **Structure:** 1-3-1 (hook, 3 points, CTA), AIDA, or a numbered list with mini-headers; tag the awareness stage.
5. **Proof:** an approved fact or a V/S stat with its ID (Real Great Roofing line exactly as approved).
6. **Bar idea and one CTA:** the UTM /book-demo link for lead-gen, or a native ask ("Comment ROUTE").
6b. Fencing is one lane in the rotation, not the default. Testimonial video ideas follow blueprints/linkedin.md (any client name needs Brennan's OK).
7. Output: slot, angle, stage, opening line, 3 points, proof + IDs, bar idea, CTA.

**Links (required):** build every gorillaworksmarketing.com link per research/utm-link-strategy.md (UPDATE Oct 14: utm_source=<platform>, utm_campaign=`<industry>_<type>` in that order, e.g. `fencing_faq`; utm_term=<article slug>; utm_medium=format; utm_content=unique post id, never reused; lowercase; blog internal and canonical links stay clean) and log each one in research/link-log.csv (`date,platform,account,post_url,link_url,utm_campaign,utm_content,industry,type,utm_term`).

Skills to reference: `gw-lead-generation` (lead-magnet naming and CTA rules, clear not clever, Proof+Promise+Plan, reading-level check, one CTA, front-loaded hook). `gw-newsletter` for newsletter issues.

## Week-1 lessons (GW Analytics, Oct 19)
- Stat-led hooks perform best, then contrarian one-liners. Hooks are A/B variants; log A or B in Notion and as -a/-b on utm_content.
- 200-280 words. No offer hooks on the company page. Never lead with the Real Great Roofing quote. The li-p1 "51%" hook is retired.
- Test link vs no link in the same slot, tagged -a and -b.
- Replies: keep the current format; ~75% pure value; soft CTA or "shoot me a DM" is fine, a URL is optional.
