# GBP post
Rules: blueprints/gbp.md. Apply `gw-content-rules` and `gw-claims-guardrails`.

## Steps
1. **Cadence check:** 1 post a day Mon/Fri/Sat/Sun and 2 a day Tue/Wed/Thu.
2. **Post:** a keyword-led first line (service + area, WA/US scope), one benefit or fact (approved only), and one CTA ("Book a free demo") with a UTM link (`utm_source=gbp`) logged in research/link-log.csv. No hashtags.
3. **Photo note:** suggest a real, geotagged photo; never a stock or scraped home photo.
4. **Review replies:** reply to every review. Thank them specifically, add no client details beyond what they wrote, and send complaints to Brennan.
5. **Review requests:** draft a short ask for a detailed review with photos. **Never offer incentives.** Brennan approves before anything is sent.
6. **Output:** the drafts plus the `gw-qa-gate` result. **Never post; Brennan does.**

Skills to reference: `gw-lead-generation` (volume, repetition, reminders over teaching, answer repeated questions as content).
