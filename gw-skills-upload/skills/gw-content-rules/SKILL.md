# Gorilla Works content rules
Sources of truth (team store): knowledge-base/00-source-of-truth.md §0/§0A (owner decisions, which override everything), research/brennan-content-rules.md, research/posting-playbook.md, and the platform blueprint in blueprints/. Where those files disagree with this summary, the files win. **Precedence (Brennan, Oct 14):** the content rules (brennan-content-rules.md) override the Company Manual wherever they conflict.

## Voice
- Write as the authority: bold, direct, confident, no hedging (§0A). Never "their site says", "describes itself as", or "positions itself as".
- Superlatives, urgency, myth-busting, and calling out channel categories are allowed (§0A). Cutesy or self-aware asides are not.
- Write how Brennan talks, to one reader ("you"). Cut adverbs, show emotion rather than naming it, vary sentence length.

## Positioning
- Lead with **custom direct mail**; barely mention bulk/EDDM/saturation.
- The customer doesn't need a mailing list: the list is built with AI from 200+ data variables plus statistical modeling.
- Differentiator: white-glove strategy plus the best data. A strategy/data/AI company that integrates with Google, Meta, and SEO, not a print shop.

## Banned lines (never, in any form)
"Yes, we ranked ourselves first", "Yes, us", "admittedly biased", "we put ourselves first", "we told you so", "read the rest anyway", "every company on it is a real option", "Obviously."

## Always
- Link gorillaworksmarketing.com. Lead-gen posts carry the UTM-tagged /book-demo link; long-form uses descriptive anchor text, never "click here".
- **Links (required):** build every gorillaworksmarketing.com link per research/utm-link-strategy.md (UPDATE Oct 14: utm_source=<platform>, utm_campaign=`<industry>_<type>` in that order, e.g. `fencing_faq`; utm_term=<article slug>; utm_medium=format; utm_content=unique post id, never reused; lowercase; blog internal and canonical links stay clean) and log each one in research/link-log.csv (`date,platform,account,post_url,link_url,utm_campaign,utm_content,industry,type,utm_term`).
- Industry mix: team/industry-mix.md (Oct 16 rotation: fencing, HVAC, roofing, siding, electrical, plumbing, other home services; at most 1-2 of every 5 on fencing). utm_campaign industries stay as listed in utm-link-strategy.md; HVAC and other new trades use home-services until Brennan adds them.
- Social mix: about 75% value / 25% lead-gen (demo). Owner decision Oct 19, 2026: supersedes earlier 75% lead-gen / 25% value. LinkedIn replies: current format, ~75% pure value; soft CTAs ("shoot me a DM", Gorilla Works without a URL) are fine.
- No hashtags, anywhere.
- **AEO/FAQ pattern:** question headings, the direct answer in the first sentence, lists/tables, self-contained sections; FAQ blocks = question + 1-3 sentence answer, approved facts only.
- Never invent data (see gw-claims-guardrails).
- Goal: site traffic, brand awareness, demo signups.
