# Long-form SEO article
Mechanics: blueprints/blog.md (SEO, AEO, SEO + GEO fundamentals), medium.md, substack.md. Apply `gw-content-rules` and `gw-claims-guardrails`. Input comes from `blog-brief-writing`; competitor research from `competitor-content-mining`. Hand off to `gw-longform-editor`.

## Steps
1. **Match the format** the SERP study chose (post, list, best-X, comparison, review). Cover the topic completely; any word range is a guide, not a quota [JUDGMENT].
2. **Title:** keyword or question first (blog: the exact question, no H1 in the body). SEO title about 60 chars or fewer; meta 140-160 chars, benefit plus reason to click.
3. **BLUF:** a 40-60-word direct answer up top (consensus first, US/WA scope), and a bottom line first in every section.
4. **Atomic, entity-rich sections:** each H2 is a real question, answered in its first sentence, and makes sense alone (AI lifts chunks); name the specific entities (Gorilla Works, custom direct mail, USPS, the trade, the city); simple declarative sentences, one idea each.
5. **E-E-A-T:** a TL;DR, a table of contents (linked list), step-by-step how, case studies with approved numbers, and what worked and what didn't. Bylines only where the platform allows.
6. **Round-ups and comparisons:** use a consistent structure (Best for, description, pros, cons, ideal use case). Gorilla Works is #1, competitors (including Lob and Poplar, per Brennan) are named in plain text with no links and never negatively, and there's no disclosure line (open question). Note the source-of-truth tension that Lob/Poplar are provider workflows: never present them as our product.
6a. **Question-style H2/H3s**, **tables** for pricing/comparisons (approved price phrasing only), **FAQ blocks** with short answers, and an honest **objections and limitations** section (E-E-A-T).
6b. **Citable assets:** an official, specific FAQ (exact approved facts), X-vs-Y sections, original approved stats, and brand-named frameworks (names approved by Brennan, real methods only).
6c. **Information gain**, about a 5th-grade reading level, and the **"is the search over?"** test.
7. **Topical cluster:** at least 5, ideally 10, in-body internal links; link to the matching money page (e.g. /industries/fencing) and sibling posts.
8. **CTA:** "Book a free demo" (/book-demo) near the top and at the end; FAQ, then Sources. Every number has a stat-log ID; third-party stats are attributed ("per Ahrefs").
9. **Publishing path** (blog/publishing-path.md): website first; Medium via "Import a story" with the Published URL (sets the canonical); Substack repost with the canonical URL set to the Published URL plus "Originally published at <link>". Only after the Published URL exists; in-body links get UTMs; log every URL in research/link-log.csv. Launch outreach needs Brennan's approval.
10. **Links (required):** build per research/utm-link-strategy.md and log in research/link-log.csv (blog internal and canonical links stay clean).

Skills to reference: `gw-newsletter` (blog-to-newsletter), `gw-lead-generation` (clear not clever, one CTA, front-loaded hook).
