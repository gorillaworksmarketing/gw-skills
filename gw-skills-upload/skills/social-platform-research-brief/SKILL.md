# Social platform research brief

Why: the copywriter and PM plan from this brief, so every number must be real and dated. Fabricated metrics lead to bad bets.

## Steps
1. **Confirm handles.** Verify each account through the platform API or the person's own linked pages, and mark it confirmed, unconfirmed, or dormant. Don't guess handles.
2. **Analyze each account.** Sample something like the last 25 original posts, excluding replies and reposts. Report the window, cadence (posts per day), median engagement (state the formula, e.g. likes+reposts+replies+quotes+bookmarks), median length, and median impressions.
3. **Top posts:** quote each one briefly and give its real URL and metrics. Name the pattern it shows (hot take plus image, one-line-per-beat story, simple math breakdown, timely alert).
4. **Hooks, formats, lengths:** summarize what the winning posts have in common.
5. **Industry conversation scan:** search each target industry, in the team's priority order, for active threads and questions.
6. **Reply targets:** list the accounts and posts to reply to, with a reason for each. Replying to other people's posts is the main X growth strategy, so give this section real effort.
7. **Post angles with A/B ideas:** give each angle two variants (e.g. number-led vs story-led hook). Every angle must obey the content rules and the QA rules.
8. **Save** the brief to the research folder as brief-YYYY-MM-DD.md, then hand off to the copywriter and add any new tactics to the tactics library.

## Rules
- Use real metrics only, and put the pull date and time (PT) at the top. If the data is thin, say so.
- Run any stat used in an angle through stat-sourcing-and-verification.
- Name competitors in plain text only, never linked.
- Log the audience questions and objections you find to the team handoffs file as blog ideas.

Skills to reference: `gw-lead-generation` (awareness levels, top-20% traits, magnet-problem discovery, collect FAQs and objections, find local trade events and groups).
