# Cold email lead research
Detail: blueprints/cold-email.md. Free public sources only. Never invent or guess. Bots never send.

## Find
1. **Sources:** Google Maps and WA/Seattle directories, company sites and About pages, GBP and public reviews, public LinkedIn professional content, local news, podcasts, trade and chamber lists. No logins, no private accounts.
2. **Industry order:** fencing, home services, B2B, electrical, holiday lighting last.
3. **Signals:** few reviews, no website ads, new permit or location, hiring, recent job or review, active posts. Pair each lead with one signal. Check our own records for prior contact.
4. **Lookalikes:** start from best clients (Real Great Roofing) and match by trade, review count, size, hiring, ad pixels.
5. **Email:** try first@, first.last@, flast@ on the company domain; verify with free checkers; flag catch-alls; bounces under 2%.

## 3-layer research (5-7 minutes with practice)
1. **Human:** common ground, headline, About, career path, time in role, past career.
2. **Company:** site, press, values, charity, podcasts, talks, articles.
3. **Space:** their words (jobs, estimates, crews, leads). Record per-industry pain notes.

## Hooks (required)
Deliver **3-5 verified hooks per person**, each with a source URL and confidence. Strongest first; the Copywriter builds the subject from it. Pattern: shared tie + inside reference + trade pain.
- **Allowed:** professional or clearly public business facts (service area, a posted job, a review reply, years in business, award, podcast, local event, chamber or alumni tie they list publicly).
- **Never:** private-feeling items (family photos, old reviews, private Facebook), sensitive topics (health, family tragedy, religion, politics, finances), guesses.
- **Don'ts:** "saw your profile", hollow ties ("Go Seminoles"), generic subjects (quick question), trivia that doesn't tie to the value.
- **No strong hook: HOLD the lead** (or move to the lower-priority list). Never send a generic subject. Quality over quantity: about 20 deeply researched leads a week to start.

## CRM fields
name, company, role, city, trade, website, email, verify status and date, signal, hook 1-3, source URL 1-3, confidence, human/company/space notes, public personal facts, touch number, last touch, next touch, nurture date, status, utm_campaign (`<industry>_<type>`), unsubscribe flag, hold reason.

## Output
CRM rows plus a hold list with reasons. Log dated changes in the blueprint changelog. Weekly review with GW Analytics: hook types that earned replies.

Skills to reference: `gw-lead-generation` (awareness levels, top-20% traits, FAQs and objections), `cold-email-copy`.
