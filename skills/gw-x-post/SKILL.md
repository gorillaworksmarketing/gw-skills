# GW X post (framework)
Platform rules: blueprints/x.md; patterns: blueprints/swipe/x.md. The copywriter delivers the framework; `gw-x-editor` writes the final version.

## Style (owner, Oct 19 2026)
Match **LinkedIn's voice more**, while still using X skills and X constraints:
- Analytical, confident, blunt — Brennan talking to one owner, not a viral-hack formula.
- Prefer a **plain keyword-led opening sentence** over a one-word or "15%." number punch.
- Complete thoughts. Short lines and blank lines are fine; avoid staccato PAIPS that reads like a template.
- Drop the rote closer stack ("No list needed… / Free demo. DM us. / 🦍") unless the post is explicitly lead-gen that day.
- Keep X limits: originals can be longer when the idea needs it (aim under ~120 or over ~250 chars; middle is weakest); replies stay under 240 chars.
- Still: no hashtags, no banned lines, no invented stats, custom direct mail first, industry rotation per team/industry-mix.md, 75% value / 25% lead-gen, ~50% daily volume until Brennan says otherwise.

## Preferred original formats (rotate)
1. **"If I ran an [trade] company as a marketing professional, this is how I'd scale"** — owner favorite (Oct 19). Walk through 3–5 concrete moves (list, offer, neighbor mail, tracking, timing). Trade fills the blank (HVAC, roofing, fence, plumber, electrician, siding, etc.).
2. Keyword-led problem → how → one next step (LinkedIn-like 1-3-1 compressed for X).
3. Myth-bust or case-study beat with an approved fact/stat ID (don't lead with Real Great Roofing).
4. Number-led is allowed when the number is the point — not every post.

## Steps
1. **Pick the idea:** from the brief, viral-post finder, handoffs, or a blog cut-down via `gw-blog-to-social`. Industry mix per team/industry-mix.md.
2. **Framework:** prefer the "If I ran…" scale play, 1-3-1, list, myth-bust, or light PAIPS (problem → why it hurts → what to do) — not rigid PAIPS every time.
3. **Hook options (3):** keyword-led sentence, counter-intuitive, or audience call-out; plain language. Number-led only when it earns the open.
4. **Key points:** specific HOW and WHAT; each number with its stat-log ID (V/S).
5. **Close:** a sharp two-sentence takeaway (LinkedIn-style bar), then at most ONE CTA on lead-gen days (soft DM or UTM /book-demo).
6. **Thread notes:** capitalized header titles without punctuation, no numbering, 4-6 relevant accounts to tag.
7. Output the framework (angle, awareness stage, hooks, points, close, CTA, stat IDs). Daily order: replies first (`social-reply`), then originals under the 50% volume cap.

**Links (required):** build every gorillaworksmarketing.com link per research/utm-link-strategy.md and log each one in research/link-log.csv.

Skills to reference: `gw-lead-generation`, `gw-linkedin-post` (for voice/structure cues to borrow).
