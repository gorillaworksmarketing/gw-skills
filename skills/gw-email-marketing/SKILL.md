# GW email marketing (lifecycle and nurture)
Detail: blueprints/email-lifecycle.md. Copy library: blueprints/email-copy-frameworks.md. Apply `gw-content-rules`, `gw-claims-guardrails`; edit with `cold-email-editing`, then `gw-qa-gate`. Bots draft; Brennan approves every send and every new sequence.

## Why
An owner who said "not now" or signed up is worth ten strangers, and stays warm only if each email is useful. So each email does one job, gives value first, and stops when they act.

## Steps
1. **Confirm permission.** Opted in, asked for info, or agreed to a check-in. If not, it belongs in `cold-email-copy`.
2. **Write the brief:** goal, segment (trade, fencing first; role; stage), trigger, key messages, offer, proof, success measure, exit conditions. Ask Brennan for anything blank.
3. **Pick the sequence** from the blueprint: welcome (sell the value of staying subscribed, then the reply-gated letter question), "not now" nurture (month 1 tip, month 3 mention, month 5 check-in), said yes but no call booked (proposal), demo no-show, reactivation (test on 10-15% of the list first), client onboarding, results and referral.
4. **Draft each email:** a reward line first; subject under about 50 characters plus one challenger; preheader 90-140 characters as a second hook; body 50-125 words for notes, 150-300 for teaching; one CTA; a P.S. for the secondary nudge; footer with the opt-out and Gorilla Works Marketing, LLC, 13120 NE 85th St Unit 123, Kirkland, WA 98033.
5. **Keep the mix:** about 75% value, 25% ask. Teaching emails carry one direct mail tip a fence owner can use this week.
6. **Proof and offers:** approved only (Real Great Roofing exact quote; "up to $500 off the first campaign"; a cold lead's own offer arm, worded exactly). Claiming an offer means booking the 15-minute call. Lead magnets, referral rewards, deadlines need Brennan's approval.
7. **Reviews:** ask every client the same neutral way, with no incentive and no filtering for happy clients.
8. **Links:** UTM per research/utm-link-strategy.md (utm_medium email, utm_campaign `<industry>_<type>`, utm_term slug), logged in research/link-log.csv.
9. **Strip AI tells** (frameworks §11 plus "I hope this finds you well", "just following up") and em dashes. Outcome first, 7th-grade level or lower. Plain text; **visual format pending from Brennan.**
10. **Speed:** positive replies get a draft within 2 minutes; auto-send waits for Brennan's reply-agent approval.

## Output
Sequence header (name, trigger, goal, timing, exits, segment), then per email: subject plus challenger, preheader, body, CTA with UTM, P.S., send day. Then to the Editor.

## Measure
Replies and demo signups by UTM decide; unsubscribes, complaints, and bounces are guardrails. Opens are not used. The list is small, so test angles and offers, not subject tweaks, and keep a small holdout on nurture when possible.

## Learning loop
Weekly with GW Analytics per the blueprint loop. Log changes in its changelog.
