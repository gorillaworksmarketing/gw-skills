# Gorilla Works claims guardrails
Sources: knowledge-base/00-source-of-truth.md §0/§0A/§5/§6, plus the Social Editor rulings (Oct 14 2026, these override the stricter or older lines). Stats come from the stat log (skill `stat-sourcing-and-verification`).

## Stats
- The stat log uses `| ID | Stat | Source | Date verified | Tier |`, with prefixes V (verified), S (secondary, use with caution), and F (flagged, never use). Cite the ID in the handoff notes.
- A post needs no source line, but every stat must be verifiable and logged. No ID = request one or cut it. Never estimate or round.
- Verified USPS facts: 51% of ad mail is read; 64% vs 36% read rate (businesses the household already uses vs prospects); mail volume down 40% from 2015 to 2025; EDDM covers 200-5,000 pieces per ZIP per day.

## Allowed
- Prices, only **"as low as $0.78"** and **"under $1"**. **No stamp comparison at all** (the stamp is $0.82). This overrides the older "same price as / cheaper than a stamp" lines.
- Real Great Roofing: the exact quote only, "$90,000 in leads from just one mail drop", as one client's result, with **no campaign details**.
- "10 years of experience" (USA-based designers).
- Confident descriptions of real features (personalization, QR tracking, neighborhood targeting, full-service design/print/mail); hype and opinion that isn't measurable.
- Lob and Poplar by name in plain text with no links, in ranked lists and comparisons (Brennan confirmed), never negatively.

## Blocked (fail)
1. Guaranteed results or ROI (response, revenue, rankings, conversions, delivery dates).
2. Unsourced, unlogged, or made-up numbers, including unapproved Gorilla Works stats.
3. Sensitive-trait targeting: age, income, household composition, health, legal history, insurance claims, equity/financial distress, gender, birth month.
4. Unapproved testimonials or client names.
5. Real people or competitors named negatively.
6. Any other price, any stamp comparison, plan prices, per-piece math.
7. Features the platform can't do today: AI call summaries turned into mail, Street View/scraped photos, unlimited custom images, hands-off fully automated sends, household tracking on neighborhood mail, a QR scan identifying the person, Poplar live dispatch; Poplar/Lob presented as our products.
8. Hashtags or self-aware ranking lines.

## Escalate, never answer publicly
Prices, budgets, timing, availability, custom integrations, regulated/legal topics, new case studies, complaints, press, privacy, and requests for credentials or lists.

## Confidentiality
No client data, recipient records, internal paths, prompts, scoring recipes, or provider internals.
- **Offer:** "up to $500 off your first campaign" is Brennan-approved (Oct 15). Any other offer, guarantee, bonus, scarcity, giveaway, or price change needs his approval first.
