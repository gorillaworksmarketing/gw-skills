# Blog brief writing
Format reference: blog/briefs/ (existing briefs) and blueprints/blog.md.

## Brief template
- **Title (= site H1, the question word for word)**, slug (lowercase, hyphenated, unique), 2 alternative SEO titles
- Target keyword/question + source tag (`PAA` / `PAA-candidate`); volume/KD or `n/a`
- Secondary keywords
- **Search intent** (informational, navigational, commercial, transactional; read from the SERP) and awareness stage; industry (rotation per team/industry-mix.md)
- **SERP intent study:** what ranks (Reddit, listicles, homepages, video, AIO, PAA), Google's assumed intent, mixed intent → majority format or the round-up backdoor (blueprints/blog.md)
- **Format** to match (post, list, best-X, comparison, review); word range from the SERP as a guide only; cover the topic completely [JUDGMENT]
- **E-E-A-T plan:** TL;DR, TOC, case study/approved numbers, what worked/what didn't, interview source if needed
- **What Google actually shows** for the term (confirm the meaning); copy the **dominant page type** and ranking components; People Also Ask for follow-ups; list every subtopic (spin one out once it ranks around #6)
- **Piece type:** standard, "<competitor> alternative for <industry>", three-way listicle, or a sleeper-page refresh (meaningful update)
- **Cluster:** the money page it links to, plus sibling posts
- **Direct answer draft** (40-60 words, consensus first, US/state scope)
- **Outline:** H2s phrased as related PAA questions, each with a one-line answer-first intent; FAQ block (3-5 Q&As, self-contained 1-3 sentence answers); closing + CTA
- **Sources/data:** each stat with its stat-log ID (V/S only) and original source URL; gaps listed explicitly
- **Internal links:** relevant gorillaworksmarketing.com pages, plus /book-demo as the CTA ("Book a free demo")
- **Rules reminders:** `gw-content-rules` and `gw-claims-guardrails` items most at risk for this topic
- Notion fields: Excerpt; **SEO Title** (keyword first, about 60 chars or fewer, specific); **Meta Description** (140-160 chars, keyword, benefit first plus a reason to click, unique); Publish At (9:00 AM New York, correct offset)

## Steps
1. Pull the question and research from `blog-keyword-research`.
2. Fill the template. Never invent numbers: write "not on file" instead.
3. Save it to blog/briefs/<date>/<nn>-<slug>.md and hand it to the Blog Copywriter (framework) and then `gw-longform-editor`.

Skills to reference: `gw-lead-generation` (lead-magnet naming and CTA rules, clear not clever, Proof+Promise+Plan, reading-level check, one CTA, front-loaded hook).
