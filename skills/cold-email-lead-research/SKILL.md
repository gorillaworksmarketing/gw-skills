# Cold email lead research
Detail: blueprints/cold-email.md (Lead research). Free public sources only. Never invent or guess. Bots never send.

## Why
Deliver fewer, verified, dated facts; the Copywriter picks the angle.

## Find
1. **Mix:** roofing first, then other trades per team/industry-mix.md. **One niche per list** [V7]; never mix trades.
2. **Sources:** Google Maps and WA directories (one by one, never bulk-scrape), company sites, GBP and reviews, public LinkedIn professional content, local news, podcasts, trade and chamber lists, public permits. No logins, no private accounts.
3. **Signals (dated):** new location or service area, permits, hiring, new owner or role, recent public post, review surge or few reviews, no ads, website status. Filter on real break points (crew size, area, role), cheapest first [V5]. Check CRM for prior contact.
4. **Lookalikes:** from best clients and positive repliers' traits [V4].
5. **Email:** first@, first.last@, flast@ on the company domain; verify with free checkers within 7 days of sending; flag catch-alls; bounces under 2%.

## Required review pull (Nov 13; part of each lead's own Maps lookup, never skipped)
One listing at a time, never bulk (Brennan, Nov 13). Read the 5 most recent Google Maps reviews; put the best-sounding qualifying one first, then up to 2 more. None qualify: check up to 10 most recent. **Qualifies:** 5 stars (any age), directly about the company (price mentions fine), word for word, and a screenshot whose listing name matches the row's company. Write the live count and rating into Review Count and Rating (the source). **Reviews** holds only Google Maps ★5 lines:
`★5 | name | relative date (captured date) | "text" | Maps URL | proof: screenshot path`
Nothing in 10: `NO QUALIFYING REVIEW (checked date)`. Never blank.

## 3-layer research (5-7 minutes)
1. **Human:** role, tenure, career path, public talks, posts.
2. **Company:** site, press, values, projects, awards, our history with them.
3. **Space:** problems the niche already talks about, in their words [V7]. Never invent one.

## Account brief (one per lead)
Fit, dated signal, priority (hot, warm, cold, skip), **3-5 hooks** each with source URL, date, confidence, a suggested angle, what not to mention, and "hook found: yes/no".
- **Allowed:** professional or clearly public business facts (service area, a posted project, a review reply, an award, a podcast, a public quote, a listed chamber or alumni tie).
- **Never:** private-feeling items, sensitive topics (health, family, religion, politics, finances, age), guesses, or conflicting numbers stated as fact (mark [U]).
- **So-what check:** each hook ties to their lead flow or neighbors around jobs, or it's weak.
- **No strong hook: HOLD the lead.** Depth beats volume.

## CRM fields
As in the blueprint, incl. verify date, signal date, hook URLs, contact source URL and date (for "how did you get my email?"), hold reason, unsubscribe flag, offer arm, problem, last campaign date (new problem and offer about a month later [V7]), utm_campaign `<industry>_<type>`.

## Output
Account briefs, CRM rows, and a hold list with reasons. After Hunter verifies a row (Ready for Summary), it's assigned, drafted, and edited within 1 day unless it fails.

## Learning loop
Weekly with GW Analytics: which hook types and signals earned positive replies. Log dated changes in the blueprint changelog.
