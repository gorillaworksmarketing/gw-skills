# GW lead magnets
Detail: blueprints/lead-magnets.md. Offer test: blueprints/cold-email.md. Apply `gw-lead-generation`, `gw-content-rules`, `gw-claims-guardrails`. Bots propose and draft; Brennan approves and publishes.

## Why
Most owners aren't ready for a call. A tangible thing they'd normally pay for earns a "yes" [V7]. If it solves the whole problem for free there is no next step; if it solves nothing real, nobody asks. Videos and audits that dump everything up front give no reason to book.

## Approved front-end offers (Brennan, Oct 30; wording exact)
A) Free landing page for direct mail ($750 value) + up to $500 in free spend
B) Free page on your website specific to direct mail ($750 value) + up to $500 off
C) Up to $500 off first campaign
D) Build a free targeted lead list ($500 value)
Flow: offer, they reply "yes", they claim it by booking a 15-minute call. First test is A vs B vs C vs D.
**Not approved:** sample postcard, list preview, mail teardown, audit, 90-second video. Don't offer them.

## Steps for a new magnet (proposal only)
1. **One narrow problem** the niche already talks about, from real evidence (PAA, groups, reviews, objections, client questions). Note the source.
2. **Match the stage:** checklist for problem-aware; the arms above for solution-aware; arm C for ready buyers.
3. **Test it:** tangible, under 10 minutes to use, can't be Googled, leaves them wanting the next step.
4. **Name it by the result,** not the format or deliverable. Two names for an A/B.
5. **Gate it right:** blog stays open; the magnet is an upgrade. In cold email, claim by reply, never a link in email 1. A form asks one qualifying question (trade, area).
6. **CTA:** the exact next action and an honest reason to act now. Deadlines or capacity limits must be real and approved.
7. **Next step:** the 15-minute claim call, or a demo link with UTM per research/utm-link-strategy.md, logged in research/link-log.csv.
8. **Claims:** approved proof only; prices only "as low as $0.78" or "under $1"; stated values only as Brennan worded them.

## Output
A one-page proposal: problem and evidence, stage, two names, gating, CTA, next step, what Brennan must approve, and the success measure (yes replies, booked calls, demo signups by UTM).

## Learning loop
Weekly with GW Analytics per the blueprint: keep, fix, or retire each offer by yes replies and booked calls. Log changes in its changelog.
