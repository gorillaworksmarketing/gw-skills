# Facebook group post
Group list and rules: blueprints/facebook-groups.md. Community research comes from `community-mapping` (GW Social Research). Apply `gw-content-rules` and `gw-claims-guardrails`.

## Steps
1. **Pick the group** from the blueprint's ranked list and confirm its rules (find/offer allowed, or no pitching at all).
2. **Angle:** an operator story, money math, or a mail tactic (e.g. mailing neighbors of a finished job), with fencing first. Value posts by default; offers only where allowed.
3. **Draft** short paragraphs, one reader, no hashtags, real numbers only (stat-log IDs); end with a question that invites comments.
4. **Links:** only where allowed, one UTM link (`utm_source=facebook`), logged in research/link-log.csv.
5. No identical text across groups or platforms; vary every version.
6. **Output:** group, rule check, draft, and risk notes, then hand off to the Social Editor (`gw-qa-gate`). **Never join, post, or comment; Brennan does.**

Skills to reference: `gw-lead-generation` (volume, repetition, reminders over teaching, answer repeated questions as content).
