# Social reply
Platform rules: blueprints/x.md and blueprints/linkedin.md. Apply `gw-content-rules` and `gw-claims-guardrails`.

## Steps
1. **Targets:** niche-relevant posts (direct mail, home services marketing, local lead gen, ad costs); prioritize accounts outperforming their size and posts under 48 hours old (daily viral list). Volume follows the blueprint (X: 5-7 replies a day before originals).
1b. **Timing:** reply to target accounts' newest posts within the first hour (targets in blueprints/x.md).
2. **Read** the post and its top replies; find a gap: a specific how, a real number (stat-log ID), a counterpoint, or an example.
3. **Draft** 1-4 short lines (X: under 240 characters), value first: no pitch, no link unless asked, no "great post", one idea. Good replies add a new insight, a related experience, a real follow-up question, or a respectful disagreement with nuance; skip engagement bait and AI tells (`gw-content-rules`).
3b. **Friend-first:** answer, then caveats, then a help offer last; identify as Brennan (Gorilla Works) when the business comes up. Keyword alerts are replied to by hand, never automated. On LinkedIn, long, thoughtful comments beat short ones.
4. Bring in the direct-mail angle only where it fits naturally. Never name competitors or people negatively; escalate prices, timing, complaints, and press (§5) instead of answering.
5. **Platform words:** "X", not "Twitter"; no hashtags; the X banned topics.
6. **Output** a table: target URL, why, draft reply, stat IDs, risk notes. Finals go through `gw-x-editor` / `gw-linkedin-editor`. **Never post, like, follow, or DM.**
6b. Daily comments also build relationships with potential linkers (publishers, journalists, niche influencers); flag good ones for the target-linker list in blueprints/backlinks.md.
7. Log questions and objections into team/handoffs.md as blog topic ideas.

Skills to reference: `gw-lead-generation` (volume, repetition, reminders over teaching, answer repeated questions as content).

Learning loop: weekly with GW Analytics (blueprints/x.md, linkedin.md): which reply types earned profile visits and follows; log there.
