# Gorilla Works content rules
Sources of truth: knowledge-base/00-source-of-truth.md §0/§0A (owner decisions win), research/brennan-content-rules.md, research/posting-playbook.md, team/industry-mix.md, the platform blueprint. Files win over this summary; content rules override the Company Manual (Oct 14).

## Voice
- Write as the authority: bold, direct, no hedging (§0A). Never "their site says" or "positions itself as".
- Superlatives, urgency, myth-busting, and calling out channel categories are allowed (§0A). Cutesy or self-aware asides are not.
- Write how Brennan talks, to one reader ("you"). Cut adverbs, vary sentence length.
- **Three-question gate** for every claim line: can the reader picture it? Can we prove it? Could a competitor say the same line? If yes to the last, make it specific to us or cut it.

## Positioning
- Lead with **custom direct mail**; barely mention bulk/EDDM/saturation.
- No mailing list needed: the list is built with AI from 200+ data variables plus statistical modeling.
- White-glove strategy plus the best data. A strategy/data/AI company that integrates with Google, Meta, and SEO, not a print shop.
- **Cold email exception [V7]:** email 1 sells the outcome (more fence jobs), not the deliverable; AI and 200+ variables wait for the call or later touches (`cold-email-copy`).

## Banned lines (never, in any form)
"Yes, we ranked ourselves first", "Yes, us", "admittedly biased", "we put ourselves first", "we told you so", "read the rest anyway", "every company on it is a real option", "Obviously."

## AI tells (cut on sight)
Em dashes; contrast reveals ("it's not X, it's Y"); negation lists ("no X, no Y"); colon reveals and self-answered questions ("The result?"); stock openers ("Here's the thing", "I'll be honest", "In today's world"); "delve, unlock, elevate, game-changer"; engagement bait ("Agree?", "Thoughts?"); wisdom-shaped closers. One fragment and one list of three per piece at most.

## Always
- Lead-gen posts carry the UTM-tagged /book-demo link; descriptive anchors, no "click here".
- **Links (required):** build per research/utm-link-strategy.md (utm_source=<platform>, utm_medium=format, utm_campaign=`<industry>_<type>`, utm_term=<slug>, unique utm_content, lowercase; blog internal and canonical links stay clean) and log each in research/link-log.csv.
- **Industry mix (Oct 23, team/industry-mix.md):** 50% of all content on every platform is fencing (about 1 in any 2 pieces). The rest rotates HVAC, roofing, siding, electrical, plumbing, landscaping, windows/doors, home services, B2B. Track daily. New trades use utm industry home-services.
- Mix: about 75% value / 25% lead-gen (Oct 19); soft CTAs are fine.
- No hashtags.
- **AEO/FAQ pattern:** question headings, answer in the first sentence, sections that stand alone; FAQ answers 1-3 sentences, approved facts only.
- Never invent data (`gw-claims-guardrails`). Goal: traffic, awareness, demo signups.

## Open question (owner)
Ranked-list disclosure: rules say none, an older memory says disclose. Use none and flag until Brennan decides.
