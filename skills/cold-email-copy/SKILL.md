# Cold email copy (Copywriter drafts, Editor checks)
Detail: blueprints/cold-email.md. Apply `gw-content-rules`, `gw-claims-guardrails`, `gw-qa-gate`. Sources are vendor training: never cite their stats (research/stat-log-pending.md, S-CE).

## Hard rules
- Brennan approves every send. Offer: up to $500 off the first campaign. Other offers, guarantees, bonuses, scarcity, giveaways need his approval.
- Approved proof only; no stamp comparison. Plain text, one ask, no em dashes, no hashtags.
- Every email has the CAN-SPAM opt-out line and postal address (Brennan supplies it).
- Only use hooks from `cold-email-lead-research` (verified, URL logged). **No hook: hold the lead, no generic subject.**

## Steps (Copywriter)
1. **Subject** from the strongest hook, ultra-personal: [shared tie] + [inside reference] + [nod to their trade pain]. Short, one-word, lowercase styles are A/B tests only.
2. **Open:** subject plus first line work as preview text: "We have yet to be properly introduced, but..." or a real observation.
3. **Transition:** "While I could talk about that all day, the real reason I'm writing is..."
4. **Value:** the challenge we solve for them, not what we do. 125 words or fewer by default.
5. **Pre-empt** the likely objection kindly in 1-2 sentences ("you may already run Facebook ads; here's how we take it further"). Never trash a competitor.
6. **CTA** A/B: interest ("Worth exploring?") vs assumptive. Default: "Do you have time over the next week or two to learn more? Tell me what works and I'll send an invite." No calendar link, no single time.
7. **Spintax** at word level, 3 variants per slot (greeting, body phrases, signature, opt-out).
8. **Cadence:** send Thu/Fri; follow-up at about 46-48 hours, same subject, 2 lines; touches 3-4 a couple of days apart with a new angle; LinkedIn connect note at touch 4-5 (custom, honest; Brennan approves; no profile views or InMails); breakup last; max 4 emails.
9. **Ask early and often;** reply to positives in minutes (speed to lead) with two times and an invite.
10. **Email as an ad:** short, curiosity-led; front-end offers (sample, list preview) need approval.
11. **Replies:** classify; use the objection bank; "not now" leads get useful articles (`gw-newsletter`), not more asks.
12. **Discovery-call opener (Brennan):** thanks, 3 relevant things, what we know, "tell me about your business and challenges, if that's okay"; open questions; let them talk.

## Editor check (Email Editor)
True (URL per hook) · not creepy, no sensitive topics · makes sense only to them · hook ties to value · claims/proof approved · CAN-SPAM line and address present · no AI tells ("it's not X, it's Y", "no X, no Y", "Quick question", "Here's the thing", colon reveals, em dashes). Output: edited copy, change log, PASS/FAIL.

## Metrics
Replies, meetings per reply, bounces, **unsubscribe and complaint rate**, demos by UTM. Tracking off. One variable per test.

Skills to reference: `gw-lead-generation` (clear not clever, Proof+Promise+Plan, one CTA, front-loaded hook, reading-level check).
Welcome or "not now" nurture notes: lettered reply question per `gw-newsletter`.
