# GW skills index
**Standing rules:** (1) At session start, check this index and reference matching skills. (2) New training files keep arriving in `cold-email/` and `lead-gen/`; at the start of each session add each new file to the matching skill (cold-email-copy, cold-email-deliverability, cold-email-lead-research, gw-newsletter, gw-lead-generation) and its blueprint. Merge; no blueprint per source. (3) Bots never send or post; Brennan approves. (4) SEO/GEO fundamentals live in blueprints/blog.md. (5) Keep each SKILL.md under about 3,500 characters; overflow goes in blueprints/.

## Shared (all bots)
- `gw-content-rules`: any GW copy; the mandatory rules and the AEO/FAQ pattern.
- `gw-claims-guardrails`: any claim, price, testimonial, or competitor mention.
- `gw-qa-gate`: the final pass/fail before approval.
- `gw-lead-generation`: anything meant to generate leads or demos.
- `gw-skills-index`: this index.

## Blog and long-form (GW Blog Research, Copywriter, Editor)
- `blog-keyword-research`: Research. Find and qualify topics from PAA by intent over volume.
- `blog-brief-writing`: Research/Copywriter. Brief with intent, title tag, meta, FAQ block.
- `longform-seo-article`: Copywriter. Draft blog, Medium, Substack articles (answer-first, E-E-A-T).
- `gw-longform-editor`: Editor. Final edit, AEO answer, links, QA.
- `ai-search-visibility`: Research/Copywriter/Editor. GEO: get named and cited in AI answers.
- `gw-newsletter`: Copywriter/Editor. Weekly issue or nurture send.

## Social (GW Social Research, Copywriter, Editor)
- `social-platform-research-brief`: Research. Weekly platform brief.
- `community-mapping`: Research. Find where owners talk.
- `competitor-content-mining`: Research. Study competitor content.
- `stat-sourcing-and-verification`: Research. Verify and log stats.
- `gw-x-post`: Copywriter. X framework.
- `gw-linkedin-post`: Copywriter. LinkedIn framework.
- `gw-blog-to-social`: Copywriter. Cut a blog into posts.
- `gw-x-editor`: Editor. Final X copy.
- `gw-linkedin-editor`: Editor. Final LinkedIn copy.
- `gw-newsletter`: Copywriter/Editor. LinkedIn newsletter issue.

## Engagement (GW Engagement)
- `social-reply`: X and LinkedIn replies.
- `reddit-comment`: Reddit comments and posts.
- `facebook-group-post`: Facebook group posts.
- `gbp-post`: Google Business Profile posts and replies.

## Backlinks (GW Backlinks)
- `link-building`: free link tactics, authority (E-E-A-T), logs.

## Analytics (GW Analytics)
- Uses `gw-lead-generation` (leading vs lagging, response time, conversion by UTM), `gw-qa-gate`, and each blueprint's learning loop.

## Cold email (GW Lead Research, Email Copywriter, Email Editor)
- `cold-email-lead-research`: Lead Research. Prospects, signals, verified hooks, CRM.
- `cold-email-copy`: Email Copywriter drafts; Email Editor runs the editor check.
- `cold-email-deliverability`: Email Editor and Grok Bot. Domain, warm-up, health, stop-loss.
- `gw-newsletter`: Email Copywriter. Nurture sends.
