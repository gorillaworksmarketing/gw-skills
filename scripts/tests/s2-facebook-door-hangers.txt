Fence companies: door hangers can work, but only when they land on the right doors.

A hanger on a random street is just more paper. A hanger on the houses next to a fence you just built is different. Those neighbors watched your crew work for days.

What makes the difference:

1. Hang them on the homes around a finished job, not across the whole town.
2. Use a real photo of the fence they've been looking at, not a stock picture.
3. Give one clear next step and one phone number you can track.
4. Follow up with a custom postcard to the same homes. Hangers fall off and get tossed with the recycling. A card in the mailbox gets carried inside.

The hanger says you were here. The mail reminds them who to call.
